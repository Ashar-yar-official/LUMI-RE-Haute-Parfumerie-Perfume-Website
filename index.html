— Our Philosophy

Scent is memory
before language.

We source only the rarest raw materials — steam-distilled Bulgarian rose, wild-harvested oud from Assam, Grasse jasmine — and blend them in small batches of no more than 500 bottles. Every flacon is numbered. Every formula is yours.

— The Craft

From Source to Skin

01

Raw Sourcing

We travel to the origin — Grasse, Mysore, Assam — and select only the finest harvest from trusted farmers.

02

Master Blending

Our in-house perfumer composes each accord by hand, testing hundreds of iterations over months.

03

Small-Batch Fill

Every bottle is filled, weighed, and sealed by hand in our Paris atelier. No automation. No shortcuts.

04

Yours, Forever

Each flacon ships in a handcrafted lacquer box with a wax seal and a numbered certificate of origin.
